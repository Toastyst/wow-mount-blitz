Mount Blitz Guide

High-volume, low-marathon path for a new player.
Get a lot of mounts fast in one focused session.
Smart time investment — big collection progress with zero cash outlay.

Midnight 2026 3–5 Hour Session Guaranteed Core Solo Friendly
Assumptions

Level ~80–90 • Almost no dungeon/raid mounts • Limited gold preferred • Can easily solo pre-DF content • Either faction • Flying unlocked

✅ Prep Checklist

  • Install the addons above and open Mount Route Planner once so it can scan your collection
  • Set Hearth to a convenient hub (capital, Dalaran, Oribos, or Silvermoon)
  • Grab free portals (mage portals, Legion Dalaran hearth if available)
  • Optional high-value: Run a few Class Trials for missing racial mounts (account progress)
  • Clear bags, repair, and have a little gold for travel/repairs
General Mechanics (difficulty, lockouts, portals)

Difficulty: Right-click your character portrait → Raid Difficulty or Dungeon Difficulty. For old raids use Legacy size (10/25). For dungeons set Heroic.

Lockouts: Most Heroic dungeons reset daily. Old raids are weekly (or flexible at max level). Many legacy lockouts are per-character — use SavedInstances.

Instance limit: ~10 instances per hour account-wide. Plan runs accordingly.

Portals: Capital city portals, Legion Dalaran hearth, Oribos, Caverns of Time portal in Tanaris.

🗺️ Single-Session Route

Geographic clusters to minimize travel. Do these in order. Click any mount for the full how-to.

01 Northrend — Guaranteed Drakes 45–75 min 100%
Black Drake (10-man) Twilight Drake (25-man) Bronze Drake

Obsidian Sanctum — Under Wyrmrest Temple, Dragonblight

/way Dragonblight 59.5 49.5 Obsidian Sanctum
Black Drake (10-player) — Full Steps
  1. Right-click portrait → Raid Difficulty → Legacy → 10-player.
  2. Enter the instance.
  3. Do not attack or pull the three Twilight Drakes (Tenebron, Shadron, Vesperon) on the outer ring. Run/fly straight past them to the center.
  4. Kill Sartharion (dies in seconds at current levels). The three drakes join but are harmless.
  5. Loot Sartharion — mount is guaranteed.
If you accidentally pull a side drake, leave and reset the instance. Extremely fast (2–5 min once inside). Same lockout as the Twilight version.
Twilight Drake (25-player) — Full Steps
  1. Right-click portrait → Raid Difficulty → Legacy → 25-player.
  2. Enter (or re-enter after the 10-man run).
  3. Exact same process: leave the three side drakes alive, kill only Sartharion.
  4. Loot for the Twilight Drake.
Do the 10-man first, leave, switch size, go back in. Still completely soloable at level 90.

Culling of Stratholme Heroic (Caverns of Time, Tanaris)

Bronze Drake — Full Timed Run
  1. Set Dungeon Difficulty to Heroic.
  2. Enter Culling of Stratholme (portal from capital or fly to Tanaris → CoT).
  3. Talk to Chromie if needed, start the event with Arthas.
  4. Clear the waves of Scourge (flags on map). There are 10 waves + bosses (Meathook, Salramm, Chrono-Lord Epoch).
  5. After Epoch, follow Arthas into the town hall / bookshelves path.
  6. Instead of going right to Mal’Ganis, go left/up the path to find the Infinite Corruptor.
  7. Kill him before the 25-minute timer expires.
  8. Mount drops for everyone present.
Timer starts when the first wave begins. At max level the whole run is trivial — AoE everything and push hard. You do not need to kill Mal’Ganis. Daily lockout.

Amani Battle Bear

Zul’Aman Heroic timed run

/way Ghostlands 81.8 64.3 Zul'Aman
Amani Battle Bear — Full Timed Run
  1. Set Dungeon Difficulty to Heroic.
  2. Enter Zul’Aman and talk to the NPC (Harrison Jones / Vol’jin equivalent) to open the gates and start the timer.
  3. Kill the first four animal bosses within the time limit (starts at 15 min; first two bosses each add 5 min → 25 min total).
  4. Recommended order for speed: Akil’zon (Eagle) → Nalorakk (Bear) → Jan’alai (Dragonhawk) → Halazzi (Lynx).
  5. After the fourth boss, free Kasha (the prisoner). She opens bags; one contains the Amani Battle Bear.
Skip as many trash packs as possible. You do not need to free the other prisoners during the run — only free Kasha at the end. Soloable and fast. Daily lockout.
03 Shadowlands Easy Chain 30–60 min High Success
Wild Seed Cradle Arboreal Gulper Shimmermist Runner Sundancer / others

Portal to Oribos → Ardenweald (and Bastion if time).

Wild Seed Cradle — Full Steps
  1. Collect the 5 gardening tools in the Garden of Night (west/central Ardenweald):
    • Gardener’s Basket ≈ /way 40.3 52.6
    • Gardener’s Hammer ≈ /way 39.7 54.4
    • Diary of the Night (book) — upper area on a bench
    • Gardener’s Flute ≈ /way 38.5 58.1
    • Gardener’s Wand ≈ /way 38.8 60.1 (under a cart)
  2. Right-click any tool in bags → combine into Twinklestar’s Gardening Toolkit.
  3. Go to Tirna Vaal (eastern Ardenweald) ≈ /way 64.0 37.4.
  4. Talk to Twinklestar and hand over the toolkit.
  5. You receive the Moonsight buff → the Cache of the Moon becomes visible directly behind her.
  6. Loot the cache for the Wild Seed Cradle.
Tools have no respawn issues; multiple players can loot. Takes 10–20 minutes total.
Arboreal Gulper — Full Steps
  1. Farm any mobs in Ardenweald until you loot an Unusually Large Mushroom (low drop rate, ~1–5%).
  2. The mushroom lasts ~20 minutes — go immediately to the Damp Loam pile (large dirt mound, northwest).
  3. Plant the mushroom.
  4. Humon’gozz (giant mushroom rare) spawns.
  5. Kill it → 100% chance at the Arboreal Gulper.
Can take 10–60+ minutes depending on mushroom luck. Group optional but not required at max level.
Shimmermist Runner — Maze Path
  1. Find the scroll “Tale of the Tangle” ≈ /way 32.5 53.0 and read it.
  2. Enter the maze and follow only the arches with blue lanterns. Avoid red or no-lantern arches.
  3. Correct path (approximate forks): Left → Right → Left → Right → Straight → Left/West into the clearing.
  4. Kill Shizgher.
  5. Click the Shimmermist Runner that was being held.
Dying does not reset progress. If you take a wrong turn the target may despawn — leave the area to reset. Takes 5–15 minutes once you know the path. Many YouTube videos show the exact route.
04 Outland Daily-Style 20–40 min Chance
Raven Lord Swift White Hawkstrider
Raven Lord (Sethekk Halls HC)
  1. Set Dungeon Difficulty to Heroic.
  2. Enter Sethekk Halls (Auchindoun, Terokkar Forest).
  3. Clear to the second boss area (or skip as much as possible).
  4. Anzu is the Heroic-only boss (summoned / appears in the middle section).
  5. Kill Anzu and loot for the chance at Raven Lord (~1%).
Short dungeon. Daily Heroic lockout. Can be combined with Magisters’ Terrace in the same Outland session. At max level the whole instance takes a few minutes.
Swift White Hawkstrider (Magisters’ Terrace HC)
  1. Set Dungeon Difficulty to Heroic.
  2. Enter Magisters’ Terrace (Isle of Quel’Danas).
  3. Clear all four bosses (Selin Fireheart → Vexallus → Priestess Delrissa → Kael’thas).
  4. Loot Kael’thas for the chance at the mount (~3%).
No key required anymore. Daily Heroic lockout. Very short and easy at current levels. Can be done every day until it drops.

Use MRP every login It re-scans and keeps the route personalized so you never farm what you already own.

Loot & vendor Old content drops plenty of vendor trash. It funds repairs and future vendor mounts.
